01 / ABOUT

A clinically anchored neuroscience lab.

TNN Lab sits at the intersection of clinical psychiatry, neuroscience and neuromodulation. Our work starts with clinically important problems and moves across levels of investigation — from phenomenology and behaviour to electrophysiology, biomarkers and experimental therapeutics.
