TNN RESEARCH ACADEMY

Open research skills for clinical & translational neuroscience.

A curated learning pathway for students, clinicians and early-career researchers. We connect you to high-quality external resources and add the practical perspective of a working translational research lab.

LEVEL 1 / RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS

Start with the skills every researcher needs.

Level 1 brings together free, reputable learning resources covering research ethics, literature searching, reference management, critical appraisal, medical statistics, scientific writing, research data management and structured research data collection.

How TNN Academy works: We curate and sequence the learning. The linked courses and resources are hosted by their original providers. TNN adds practical context, learning objectives and exercises.

016 hours • Self-paced

Good Clinical Practice

Understand the ethical and scientific principles that protect research participants and support high-quality clinical trials.

TNN Perspective

GCP is not just a certificate. It is the framework behind informed consent, protocol adherence, participant safety, documentation and responsible conduct of a clinical trial.

Start with
  • Participant protection
  • Protocol compliance
  • Investigator responsibilities
  • Research documentation

02Free • Lecture series

Research Ethics & Human Subjects

Understand the ethical principles and practical issues involved in research with human participants, including informed consent, risk–benefit assessment and ethics review.

TNN Perspective

Ethics is not a form to complete before recruitment. It shapes how we select participants, obtain consent, protect privacy, handle risks and respond when the research plan meets a real person.

Learn about
  • Informed consent
  • IRBs / Ethics Committees
  • Risk–benefit assessment
  • Fair participant selection
Start NIH Ethics Course ↗

Free NIH lecture series. Recordings from current and previous courses are available; certificate requirements apply if you choose to pursue one.

Research Data Management

Learn how to organise, document, protect, store and preserve research data throughout the life of a project.

TNN Perspective

Good data management starts before the first participant. Clear variable definitions, file organisation, documentation, security and version control prevent problems that are painful—or impossible—to fix later.

Learn about
  • Data management plans
  • Data organisation & documentation
  • Storage, security & backup
  • Data protection and sharing
Start Edinburgh MANTRA ↗

Free, open research-data-management training from the University of Edinburgh.

09~1–2 hours • Self-paced

PubMed & Literature Searching

Learn to turn a clinical question into a structured search using PubMed, MeSH and evidence-based search strategies.

TNN Perspective

A good search starts before you type into PubMed. Define the question, identify concepts, use MeSH where appropriate, then combine terms systematically. Avoid relying on a single keyword or a vague Google search.

Learn to
  • Frame searchable questions
  • Use MeSH terms
  • Build Boolean searches
  • Find and follow relevant evidence
Start NLM PubMed Training ↗

Zotero & Reference Management

Build an organized research library and manage papers, PDFs, citations and bibliographies efficiently.

TNN Perspective

Reference management should be a lab-wide system, not six separate personal folders. Consistent organization and shared libraries make collaborative writing dramatically easier.

Learn to
  • Collect and organize papers
  • Manage PDFs
  • Insert citations
  • Build bibliographies

04~1–2 hours • Self-paced

Critical Appraisal

Learn to judge whether research findings are trustworthy, meaningful and applicable to the question you are asking.

TNN Perspective

Do not turn critical appraisal into a checklist score. Ask three questions: Can I trust the result? What exactly does it show? Does it matter clinically?

Focus on
  • Risk of bias
  • Effect size and precision
  • Validity and applicability
  • Authors' conclusions vs data
Start Cochrane Appraisal Videos ↗

Free seven-video critical-appraisal series. CASP checklists remain available as the practical appraisal framework.

0516 hours • Self-paced

Medical Statistics

Develop a practical foundation in the statistical concepts used to interpret medical and clinical research.

TNN Perspective

Statistics is not about hunting for a p-value below 0.05. Understand the clinical question, effect size, uncertainty and assumptions before choosing a test.

Build foundations in
  • Risk and odds
  • Confidence intervals
  • Hypothesis testing
  • Chi-square and Fisher's exact test
Start OpenLearn Medical Statistics ↗
0616+ hours • Self-paced

Scientific Writing

Learn how to structure and communicate scientific work clearly, from manuscript planning to abstracts and technical writing.

TNN Perspective

A manuscript is an argument supported by data. Keep the research question visible, make the methods reproducible, report results without embellishment and never let the discussion claim more than the study can support.

Learn about
  • Manuscript structure
  • Abstract writing
  • Technical language
  • Publication-oriented writing
Start Elsevier Researcher Academy ↗
07~1–1.5 hours • Starter set

REDCap

Learn the fundamentals of structured research data collection, CRFs, data dictionaries and longitudinal projects.

TNN Perspective

A database designed badly becomes a statistical headache later. Think about variables, coding, validation, missing data and longitudinal structure before the first participant is enrolled.

Learn about
  • Online Designer
  • Data Dictionary
  • Data Entry
  • Longitudinal projects
